Statewide programs

ADRS Vocational Rehabilitation (equipment for work)

Alabama Department of Rehabilitation Services

Statewide, AL

Can pay for medical equipment, assistive devices and job-site changes a person with a disability needs to get or keep a job or go to school.

1-800-441-7607
Who
Alabamians with a disability who want to work
Good to know
Free. A rehab counselor builds a work plan with you.

United Way 2-1-1

Anywhere

Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211.

2-1-1
Who
Anyone

Statewide, AL

Job search, resume help and training referrals at 57 career centers statewide.

Senior Community Service Employment Program (SCSEP)

Alabama Department of Senior Services

Statewide, AL

Paid part-time job training (about 20 hours a week) to help older workers into regular jobs.

1-800-243-5463
Who
Age 55+, unemployed, income under 125% of poverty
Email
ageline@adss.alabama.gov

Ticket to Work

Social Security Administration

Federal

Free job training, placement and benefits counseling so people on disability benefits can work.

1-866-968-7842
Who
Ages 18-64 receiving SSDI or SSI based on disability
Email
TicketToWork@ssa.gov

Alabama Department of Human Resources

Statewide, AL

Apply online or at the county DHR office

Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)

Social Security Administration

Federal

Monthly benefits for people who can't work because of a disability and have enough work credits.

1-800-772-1213
Who
