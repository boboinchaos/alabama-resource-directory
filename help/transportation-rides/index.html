ALDOT rural transit office

Alabama Department of Transportation

Statewide, AL

State office for rural transit. Call to find the ride provider for any county.

334-242-6766
Who
Anyone looking for rural transit

DAV vans to the Birmingham VA

Disabled American Veterans / Birmingham VA

Statewide, AL

Free volunteer van rides to Birmingham VA appointments. Book 48 hours ahead on I-65 routes, a week on others.

205-933-8101 ext. 5477
Who
Veterans with no other transportation
WebsiteWrong info? Tell us

Medicaid rides to medical appointments (NET)

Alabama Medicaid Agency

Statewide, AL

Pays for rides, or reimburses trips, to Medicaid-covered medical, dental and hospital appointments. Press option 4.

1-800-362-1504
Who
Full Medicaid coverage; request at least 5 days ahead
WebsiteWrong info? Tell us

VA Veterans Transportation Service

Birmingham VA Medical Center

Statewide, AL

VA-run rides to appointments within about 50 miles of the Birmingham VA. Ask for a VTP representative.

205-933-8101
Who
Enrolled veterans; request 14 days ahead

United Way 2-1-1

Anywhere

Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211.

Anyone
