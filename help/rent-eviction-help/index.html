Statewide programs

Alabama DHR - Foster Youth to Independence (FYI) Housing Vouchers

Statewide (AL)

Time-limited (up to 3 years) Housing Choice Voucher for youth leaving foster care who are homeless or at risk, referred by DHR to the local housing authority.

(334) 242-9500
Who
Youth 18 to under 25 who left foster care at 16+ (or will within 90 days) and are homeless or at risk
Email
ILP@dhr.alabama.gov
Good to know
Referral comes through DHR/ILP worker, not directly to the PHA.

Presbyterian Home for Children

Statewide (AL)

Talladega campus: Moderate Care home for teen girls referred by DHR, Transition to Adult Living (women 19-24), Secure Dwellings housing for homeless children with female caregivers, Family Bridges reunification, therapeutic foster care.

256-362-2114
Who
Teen girls in DHR care; young women 19-24; homeless mothers/female caregivers with children 0-17
Where
905 Ashland Hwy, Talladega, AL 35160
Good to know

Alabama DHR - Independent Living Program (Chafee)

Statewide (AL)

Independent living services for foster youth age 14+ in every county: life skills, transition planning, ILP funding, aftercare for youth 18-20 who left care and return to request services, D.R.E.A.M. youth council.

(334) 242-9500
Who
Youth 14+ in DHR care; youth 18-20 who were in foster care on/after their 18th birthday
Where
50 N. Ripley St., Montgomery, AL 36130

📋 What to bring

Most programs ask for:

  • Photo ID
  • The past-due bill or shutoff notice
  • Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters)
  • Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home
