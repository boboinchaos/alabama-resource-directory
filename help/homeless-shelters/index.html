Alabama DHR - Foster Youth to Independence (FYI) Housing Vouchers

Statewide (AL)

Time-limited (up to 3 years) Housing Choice Voucher for youth leaving foster care who are homeless or at risk, referred by DHR to the local housing authority.

(334) 242-9500
Who
Youth 18 to under 25 who left foster care at 16+ (or will within 90 days) and are homeless or at risk
Email
ILP@dhr.alabama.gov
Good to know
Referral comes through DHR/ILP worker, not directly to the PHA.
