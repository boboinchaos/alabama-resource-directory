Statewide programs

Amputee Coalition: National Limb Loss Resource Center

Amputee Coalition

National (serves Alabama)

Free help for people with limb loss: what a prosthesis should cost, insurance appeals, a healthcare navigator, trained peer visitors and local support groups.

1-888-267-5669
Who
Anyone with limb loss or limb difference, and their families
Good to know
Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern.
WebsiteWrong info? Tell us

Shriners Children's

Free pediatric prosthetics and orthotics

National (serves Alabama)

Specialty orthopedic care for kids, including custom prosthetic limbs, braces and wheelchair seating, regardless of the family's ability to pay.

1-800-237-5055
Who
Children from birth to 18
Good to know
No doctor referral needed. Alabama families are usually seen at Shriners Children's Shreveport, Greenville or Lexington; local Shrine clubs often help with travel.
WebsiteWrong info? Tell us

United Way 2-1-1

Anywhere

Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211.

2-1-1
Who
Anyone
