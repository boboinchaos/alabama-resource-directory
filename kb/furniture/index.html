Alabama resources for: Furniture or beds

Help with furniture or beds in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • Sleep in Heavenly Peace — Phone: Apply online — Builds and delivers free twin beds with mattress and bedding for children. — Who qualifies: Children 3-17 in a zip code with an active chapter; long waitlist — Website: https://shpbeds.org
