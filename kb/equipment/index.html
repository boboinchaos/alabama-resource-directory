  • ADRS Vocational Rehabilitation (equipment for work) — Phone: 1-800-441-7607 — Can pay for medical equipment, assistive devices and job-site changes a person with a disability needs to get or keep a job or go to school. — Who qualifies: Alabamians with a disability who want to work — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://www.rehab.alabama.gov/services/vr/vr
