Alabama resources for: Broken plumbing or leak

Help with broken plumbing or leak in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • H2O Foundation — Phone: 205-244-4390 — Up to $500 toward a Central Alabama Water bill and up to $1,000 for small plumbing repairs. Can help with deposits to restore service (board approval). — Who qualifies: Central Alabama Water customers who meet income and situation guidelines
  • USDA Section 504 home repair loans and grants — Phone: 334-279-3440 — 1% loans up to $40,000 and grants up to $10,000 to fix homes and remove health and safety hazards. — Who qualifies: Very-low-income rural homeowners; grants only for age 62+ — Website: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants
  • Alabama Habitat for Humanity — Phone: 334-737-6401 — Connects people to local Habitat affiliates for home repair and affordable homeownership. — Who qualifies: Low-income households in a local affiliate's area — Website: https://alabamahabitat.org — Email: support@alabamahabitat.org
  • Rebuilding Together Central Alabama — Phone: (334) 625-9062 — Free home repairs to make homes safe and healthy, including accessibility. — Who qualifies: Low-income homeowners in its service area — Website: https://rtbama.org
