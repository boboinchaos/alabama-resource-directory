Alabama resources for: Power bill / shutoff notice

Help with power bill / shutoff notice in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • LIHEAP energy assistance — Phone: 205-287-0139 — Federal energy help paid straight to the power or gas company. Every Alabama county is served by a Community Action Agency that takes the applications. — Who qualifies: Income at or under 150% of the federal poverty level. Heating season: Oct 1 – Apr 30. — Address: 116 Chilton Place, Clanton 35045 — Website: http://www.capmal.org/
  • Project SHARE — Phone: (205) 328-2420 — Emergency help with power bills for older and disabled Alabamians, funded by Alabama Power customers and electric co-ops. — Who qualifies: Age 60+ or a person with a disability
  • Weatherization — Phone: 205-287-0139 — Free home energy upgrades like insulation and air sealing that lower bills going forward. The state line routes them to their county's agency. — Who qualifies: Income at or under 200% of the federal poverty level — Address: 116 Chilton Place, Clanton 35045 — Website: http://www.capmal.org/
  • Alabama Power low-income bill credit — Phone: 1-800-245-2244 — A $14.50 monthly credit on the power bill. — Who qualifies: Alabama Power customers receiving SSI or Medicaid for Low Income Families — Website: https://alabamapower.com/paymentassistance
  • Alabama Power Special Needs Assistance — Phone: 1-800-245-2244 — Due dates matched to benefit check dates, Braille or large-print bills, and services for deaf customers. — Who qualifies: Alabama Power customers with special needs or on monthly government checks — Website: https://alabamapower.com/paymentassistance
  • ABC Trust energy help — Phone: Ask the LIHEAP agency above — Emergency help with energy bills, given out through the county LIHEAP agency. — Who qualifies: Low-income households or those in temporary hardship — Website: http://powerofgood.com/abc-trust/
  • Alabama electric cooperatives (AREA) — Phone: 1-800-410-2732 — Points co-op members to their local electric cooperative for billing help. — Who qualifies: Members of Alabama electric cooperatives — Website: https://www.areapower.coop/our-members/co-ops
  • Public Service Commission consumer complaints — Phone: 1-800-392-8050 — Takes complaints about regulated power, gas, water and phone utilities after the customer has tried the utility. — Who qualifies: Customers of PSC-regulated utilities — Website: https://psc.alabama.gov/file-a-complaint/
