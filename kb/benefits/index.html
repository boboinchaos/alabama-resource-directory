Alabama resources for: Social Security, SSI / SSDI, Medicaid

Help with social security, ssi / ssdi, medicaid in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • SNAP food assistance — Phone: (334) 358-5000 — Monthly grocery benefits, applied for at the county DHR office or online. Every dollar not spent on food can go toward the bill. — Who qualifies: Households under SNAP income limits — Address: 203 N Court St, Prattville 36067 — Website: https://mydhr.alabama.gov — Email: Autauga@dhr.alabama.gov
  • Supplemental Security Income (SSI) — Phone: 1-800-772-1213 — Monthly cash for people with very low income and few resources who are 65+, blind, or disabled. — Who qualifies: Age 65+, blind or disabled, with limited income and resources — Website: https://www.ssa.gov/ssi/
  • Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) — Phone: 1-800-772-1213 — Monthly benefits for people who can't work because of a disability and have enough work credits. — Who qualifies: Workers with a qualifying disability and enough work history — Website: https://www.ssa.gov/applyfordisability/
  • Extra Help (Medicare drug costs) — Phone: 1-800-772-1213 — Lowers Medicare prescription drug premiums, deductibles and copays. — Who qualifies: People with Medicare and limited income and resources — Website: https://www.ssa.gov/extrahelp
  • Ticket to Work — Phone: 1-866-968-7842 — Free job training, placement and benefits counseling so people on disability benefits can work. — Who qualifies: Ages 18-64 receiving SSDI or SSI based on disability — Website: https://choosework.ssa.gov — Email: TicketToWork@ssa.gov
  • 1-800-MEDICARE — Phone: 1-800-633-4227 — Answers Medicare coverage, enrollment, billing and plan questions. — Who qualifies: Medicare beneficiaries and caregivers — Website: https://www.medicare.gov
  • Alabama SHIP (Medicare counseling) — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — Free, unbiased counseling on Medicare, Part D, Medicare Savings Programs, Medicaid and billing problems. — Who qualifies: Medicare beneficiaries, families and caregivers — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov/ship/ — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov
  • Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, QI-1) — Phone: 1-800-362-1504 — Pays Medicare Part B premiums and, for QMB, Part A premium, deductibles and coinsurance. — Who qualifies: Low-income people who have or qualify for Medicare — Website: https://medicaid.alabama.gov/CONTENT/3.0_Apply/3.2_Qualifying/3.2.6_Help_Paying_Medicare.aspx
  • Alabama Medicaid — Phone: 1-800-362-1504 — Health coverage for eligible low-income children, families, pregnant women, elderly and disabled Alabamians. — Who qualifies: Low-income residents meeting program rules — Website: https://medicaid.alabama.gov
  • Family Assistance (TANF) and JOBS — Phone: Apply online or at the county DHR office — Temporary cash help plus job training for low-income families with children. — Who qualifies: Low-income families with children under 18 — Website: https://dhr.alabama.gov/family-assistance/
  • Lifeline phone and internet discount — Phone: 1-800-234-9473 — Monthly discount on phone or internet service. — Who qualifies: Income at or below 135% of poverty, or on Medicaid, SNAP, SSI, federal housing aid or Veterans Pension — Website: https://www.lifelinesupport.org — Email: LifelineSupport@usac.org
  • Alabama Ageline / ADRC — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — One number that connects older adults, people with disabilities and caregivers to local aging services and benefits help. — Who qualifies: Older adults, people with disabilities, caregivers — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov
  • Eldercare Locator — Phone: 1-800-677-1116 — National line that connects older adults and caregivers to local aging services. — Who qualifies: Older adults and caregivers — Website: https://eldercare.acl.gov — Email: eldercarelocator@USAging.org
  • AlabamaABLE savings account — Phone: 1-833-711-2253 — Tax-advantaged savings for disability expenses that doesn't count against SSI or Medicaid limits. — Who qualifies: People with disabilities meeting ABLE onset-age rules — Website: https://www.alabamaable.gov
  • Legal Services Alabama — Phone: 1-866-456-4995 — Free civil legal help: housing, benefits, consumer, family and elder law. — Who qualifies: Low-income households; some help for 60+ regardless of income — Website: https://legalservicesalabama.org
  • Volunteer Lawyers Program — Phone: 1-888-857-8571 — Free civil legal help from volunteer attorneys. Jefferson County residents use Volunteer Lawyers Birmingham: 205-250-5198. — Who qualifies: Low-income residents — Website: https://alabar.org/for-the-public/get-legal-help
  • Alabama Legal Help — Phone: Online only — Free online legal information, forms and self-help guides. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.alabamalegalhelp.org
  • Alabama Free Legal Answers — Phone: Online only — Post a civil legal question online and a volunteer lawyer answers. — Who qualifies: Low-income Alabama residents — Website: https://alabama.freelegalanswers.org
  • VA benefits — Phone: 1-800-827-1000 — Disability compensation, pension, Aid & Attendance and other benefits for veterans and survivors. — Who qualifies: Veterans, dependents and survivors — Website: https://www.benefits.va.gov/benefits/
