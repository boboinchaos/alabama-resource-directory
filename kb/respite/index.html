Alabama resources for: Respite or caregiver help

Help with respite or caregiver help in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • SAIL waiver — Phone: 1-844-602-7245 — In-home supports so people with significant disabilities can keep living at home. — Who qualifies: Adults with significant physical disabilities who qualify for Medicaid waiver services
  • Medicaid in-home care waivers — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — Pays for in-home care (personal care, homemaker, respite, adult day, meals) so people can stay out of nursing homes. — Who qualifies: Elderly or disabled, Medicaid-eligible, at nursing-home level of care — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov/medicaid-waiver-programs/ — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov
  • Alabama Ageline / ADRC — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — One number that connects older adults, people with disabilities and caregivers to local aging services and benefits help. — Who qualifies: Older adults, people with disabilities, caregivers — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov
  • Alabama CARES caregiver support — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — Training, information and respite care for family caregivers. — Who qualifies: Caregivers of adults 60+; relatives 55+ raising children — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov/2020/06/01/alabama-cares-and-seniorrx/
  • Eldercare Locator — Phone: 1-800-677-1116 — National line that connects older adults and caregivers to local aging services. — Who qualifies: Older adults and caregivers — Website: https://eldercare.acl.gov — Email: eldercarelocator@USAging.org
  • Alabama Lifespan Respite vouchers — Phone: 256-859-8300 — Reimburses family caregivers for respite from a provider they choose. — Who qualifies: Full-time unpaid caregivers (40+ hrs/week) of someone with a chronic illness or disability who get no other respite — Website: https://alabamarespite.org/ — Email: al.respite@ucphuntsville.org
  • Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline — Phone: 1-800-272-3900 — Round-the-clock dementia care advice, referrals and caregiver support groups. — Who qualifies: Anyone affected by Alzheimer's or dementia — Website: https://www.alz.org/al/about
  • HEARTS Respite for children — Phone: (256) 852-5600 — Home-based respite vouchers for families of children with special needs. — Who qualifies: Caregivers of children birth to 19 with disabilities or special medical needs — Website: https://www.ucphuntsville.org/hearts-respite/ — Email: swright@ucphuntsville.org
  • Alabama Head Injury Foundation — Phone: 1-800-433-8002 — Respite, caregiver programs, support groups and camps for people with brain injuries and families. — Who qualifies: People with traumatic brain injury and their caregivers — Website: https://www.ahif.org/
  • Alabama Head and Spinal Cord Injury Helpline — Phone: 1-888-879-4706 — Information, resource coordination and service linkage for people with a brain or spinal cord injury, including help finding equipment. — Who qualifies: Alabama residents with a traumatic brain injury or spinal cord injury — Website: https://www.rehab.alabama.gov
