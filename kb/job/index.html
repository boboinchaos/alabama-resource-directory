Alabama resources for: Need a job or steady income

Help with need a job or steady income in Alabama. 77 programs in total. Statewide programs first, then county-specific programs by county.

Statewide programs for job and income help (any Alabama county)

  • ADRS Vocational Rehabilitation (equipment for work) — Phone: 1-800-441-7607 — Can pay for medical equipment, assistive devices and job-site changes a person with a disability needs to get or keep a job or go to school. — Who qualifies: Alabamians with a disability who want to work — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://www.rehab.alabama.gov/services/vr/vr
  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • AlabamaWorks Career Centers — Phone: 334-309-9000 — Job search, resume help and training referrals at 57 career centers statewide. — Who qualifies: Any job seeker — Serves: every Alabama county — Email: helpdesk.alabamaworks@workforce.alabama.gov — Website: https://workforce.alabama.gov/job-seekers/career-centers/
  • Senior Community Service Employment Program (SCSEP) — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — Paid part-time job training (about 20 hours a week) to help older workers into regular jobs. — Who qualifies: Age 55+, unemployed, income under 125% of poverty — Serves: every Alabama county — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov/scsep/
  • Ticket to Work — Phone: 1-866-968-7842 — Free job training, placement and benefits counseling so people on disability benefits can work. — Who qualifies: Ages 18-64 receiving SSDI or SSI based on disability — Serves: every Alabama county — Email: TicketToWork@ssa.gov — Website: https://choosework.ssa.gov
  • Unemployment Compensation — Phone: 1-866-234-5382 — Weekly payments to workers who lost a job through no fault of their own. — Who qualifies: Workers unemployed through no fault of their own — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://adol.alabama.gov/divisions/unemployment-compensation/
  • Family Assistance (TANF) and JOBS — Phone: Apply online or at the county DHR office — Temporary cash help plus job training for low-income families with children. — Who qualifies: Low-income families with children under 18 — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://dhr.alabama.gov/family-assistance/
  • Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) — Phone: 1-800-772-1213 — Monthly benefits for people who can't work because of a disability and have enough work credits. — Who qualifies: Workers with a qualifying disability and enough work history — Serves: every Alabama county — Website: https://www.ssa.gov/applyfordisability/
