Alabama resources for: Transportation / rides

Help with transportation / rides in Alabama, from the DRR Resource Navigator. Statewide programs first, then local resources by county.

Statewide programs (any Alabama county)

  • United Way 2-1-1 — Phone: 2-1-1 — Free, 24/7 referral line. Finds the smaller church and community funds near their address that open up week to week. Also 1-888-421-1266, or text a ZIP code to 898-211. — Who qualifies: Anyone — Website: https://www.211connectsalabama.org
  • Alabama Ageline / ADRC — Phone: 1-800-243-5463 — One number that connects older adults, people with disabilities and caregivers to local aging services and benefits help. — Who qualifies: Older adults, people with disabilities, caregivers — Website: https://alabamaageline.gov — Email: ageline@adss.alabama.gov
  • Eldercare Locator — Phone: 1-800-677-1116 — National line that connects older adults and caregivers to local aging services. — Who qualifies: Older adults and caregivers — Website: https://eldercare.acl.gov — Email: eldercarelocator@USAging.org
  • VA health care — Phone: 1-877-222-8387 — Health care enrollment for eligible veterans. — Who qualifies: Veterans meeting service and discharge requirements — Website: https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/
  • Medicaid rides to medical appointments (NET) — Phone: 1-800-362-1504 — Pays for rides, or reimburses trips, to Medicaid-covered medical, dental and hospital appointments. Press option 4. — Who qualifies: Full Medicaid coverage; request at least 5 days ahead — Website: https://medicaid.alabama.gov/content/4.0_Programs/4.5_Transportation.aspx
  • ALDOT rural transit office — Phone: 334-242-6766 — State office for rural transit. Call to find the ride provider for any county. — Who qualifies: Anyone looking for rural transit — Website: https://www.aldot.gov/programs/ruralTransit.html
  • DAV vans to the Birmingham VA — Phone: 205-933-8101 ext. 5477 — Free volunteer van rides to Birmingham VA appointments. Book 48 hours ahead on I-65 routes, a week on others. — Who qualifies: Veterans with no other transportation — Website: https://www.va.gov/birmingham-health-care/dav-vans-transportation-for-veterans/
  • VA Veterans Transportation Service — Phone: 205-933-8101 — VA-run rides to appointments within about 50 miles of the Birmingham VA. Ask for a VTP representative. — Who qualifies: Enrolled veterans; request 14 days ahead — Website: https://www.va.gov/birmingham-health-care/locations/birmingham-va-medical-center/
