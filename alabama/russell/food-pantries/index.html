Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Russell County, Alabama?
Start with Kingdom Builders Foundation Ministries and House of Restoration ((334) 214-5522), Open Door Community House - Homeless Program (Columbus GA) ((706) 323-5518) and BeYond Expectations Community Outreach ((334) 384-1340). This page lists 30 programs that serve Russell County: 24 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Russell County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Kingdom Builders Foundation Ministries and House of Restoration serves: Homeless people in Phenix City.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Bullock, Lee that may serve you.
