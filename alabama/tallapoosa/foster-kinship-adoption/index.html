Common questions

Where can I get foster care, kinship or adoption support in Tallapoosa County, Alabama?
Start with Tallapoosa County DHR - Family Services (foster care, kinship, adoption) ((256) 825-3700). This page lists 37 programs that serve Tallapoosa County: 9 local or regional and 28 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for foster care, kinship or adoption support in Tallapoosa County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Tallapoosa County DHR - Family Services (foster care, kinship, adoption) serves: Residents of Tallapoosa County; relatives caring for children; foster/adoptive applicants.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for foster care, kinship or adoption support near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Chambers, Clay, Coosa that may serve you.
