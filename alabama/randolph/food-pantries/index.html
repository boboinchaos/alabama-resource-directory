Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Randolph County, Alabama?
Start with Christian Service Center (Roanoke and Wedowee) ((334) 863-4226), Community Life Church Roanoke Food Bank ((334) 863-5433) and First United Methodist Church of Wedowee ((256) 357-2214). This page lists 20 programs that serve Randolph County: 14 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Randolph County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Christian Service Center (Roanoke and Wedowee) serves: Randolph County residents in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Chambers, Clay, Cleburne that may serve you.
