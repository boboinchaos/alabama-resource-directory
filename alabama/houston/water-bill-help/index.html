Catholic Social Services - Dothan

Emergency Assistance Ministry

Local · Houston County, AL

By appointment Mon-Fri 9am-3pm: food, clothing, furniture and appliances, household and hygiene items, and limited financial help with rent, utilities, medicine and transportation.

(334) 793-3601
Who
Houston County residents in crisis; no religious requirement
Where
557 E. Main St., Dothan 36301
