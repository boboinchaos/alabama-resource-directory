Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Henry County, Alabama?
Start with Catholic Social Services of Dothan ((334) 793-3601) and The Salvation Army Dothan Corps ((334) 792-1911). This page lists 4 programs that serve Henry County: 3 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Henry County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Catholic Social Services of Dothan serves: Anyone in crisis regardless of religion; bring photo ID for adults, lease/mortgage, current bills, proof of income, SS cards for household; limited, usually one-time help.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Dale, Houston that may serve you.
