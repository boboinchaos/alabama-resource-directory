Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Henry County, Alabama?
Start with Balkum Baptist Church Food Pantry ((334) 693-0215), Christ Temple Outreach ((334) 787-5713) and First Baptist Church of Headland Food Pantry ((334) 693-3220). This page lists 23 programs that serve Henry County: 17 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Henry County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Balkum Baptist Church Food Pantry serves: People in need in Henry County.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Dale, Houston that may serve you.
