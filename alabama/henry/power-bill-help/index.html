Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Henry County, Alabama?
Start with Project SHARE intake - Henry County Department of Human Resources (334-585-4128). This page lists 19 programs that serve Henry County: 9 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Henry County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Project SHARE intake - Henry County Department of Human Resources serves: Low-income residents age 60+ and/or disabled; bring ID, bill/cutoff notice, proof of income.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Dale, Houston that may serve you.
