Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Lee County, Alabama?
Start with Auburn Community Development (Auburn Housing Authority Food Pantry) (334-821-2262 ext. 208), Auburn Opelika SDA Church (334-758-4272) and Auburn United Methodist Church - AUMC Food and Clothes Pantry ((334) 826-8800). This page lists 35 programs that serve Lee County: 29 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Lee County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Auburn Community Development (Auburn Housing Authority Food Pantry) serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Chambers, Macon, Russell that may serve you.
