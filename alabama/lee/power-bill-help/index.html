Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Lee County, Alabama?
Start with Auburn United Methodist Church - AUMC Food and Clothes Pantry ((334) 826-8800), Presbyterian Community Ministry ((334) 887-3777) and SVdP - St. Mary Conference (Opelika) ((334) 749-8359). This page lists 25 programs that serve Lee County: 15 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Lee County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Auburn United Methodist Church - AUMC Food and Clothes Pantry serves: Lee County families.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Chambers, Macon, Russell that may serve you.
