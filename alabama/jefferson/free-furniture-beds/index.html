Common questions

Where can I get free furniture or a bed in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with Catholic Center of Concern ((205) 786-4388), Catholic Center of Concern - Birmingham ((205) 786-4388) and Community Furniture Bank ((205) 942-2727). This page lists 13 programs that serve Jefferson County: 11 local or regional and 2 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free furniture or a bed in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Catholic Center of Concern serves: People of all faiths in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free furniture or a bed near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
