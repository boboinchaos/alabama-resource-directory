Common questions

Where can I get help with roof and home repairs in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with City of Birmingham Critical Repair Program ((205) 581-3117), Renew Birmingham ((205) 201-4275) and Christian Service Mission ((205) 252-9906). This page lists 17 programs that serve Jefferson County: 12 local or regional and 5 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with roof and home repairs in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, City of Birmingham Critical Repair Program serves: City of Birmingham homeowners selected through the application.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with roof and home repairs near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
