Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with LIHEAP Crisis ((256) 899-8760), AIDS Alabama (205-324-9822) and Urban Ministry ((205) 781-0517). This page lists 33 programs that serve Jefferson County: 25 local or regional and 8 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, LIHEAP Crisis serves: Income at or under 150% of the federal poverty level. Heating season: Oct 1 – Apr 30.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
