Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with Firehouse Ministries emergency shelter ((205) 252-9571 ext. 21), First Light emergency shelter ((205) 323-4277) and Jimmie Hale Mission Jessie's Place ((205) 323-0170). This page lists 39 programs that serve Jefferson County: 27 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Brother Bryan Mission serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
