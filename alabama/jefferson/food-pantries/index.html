Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with Meals on Wheels (Jefferson County) (1-800-243-5463), Adamsville Seventh-day Adventist Church pantry (205-674-1043) and Adventist Community Services, Ephesus (205-252-7912). This page lists 143 programs that serve Jefferson County: 138 local or regional and 5 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Brother Bryan Mission serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
