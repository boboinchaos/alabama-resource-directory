Common questions

Where can I get help with a water bill or shutoff in Jefferson County, Alabama?
Start with AIDS Alabama (205-324-9822), Urban Ministry ((205) 781-0517) and American Red Cross ((205) 439-7800). This page lists 21 programs that serve Jefferson County: 17 local or regional and 4 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with a water bill or shutoff in Jefferson County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, AIDS Alabama serves: Household with an HIV+ member, within 300% of the federal poverty level, with an open AIDS Alabama case file.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with a water bill or shutoff near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Blount, Shelby that may serve you.
