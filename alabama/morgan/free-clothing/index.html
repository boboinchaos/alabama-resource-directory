Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Morgan County, Alabama?
Start with Salvation Army of Decatur (Center of Hope) ((256) 353-2822), Tennessee Valley Outreach ((256) 350-5226) and Beltline Church of Christ Benevolence ((256) 353-1876). This page lists 12 programs that serve Morgan County: 11 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Morgan County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Salvation Army of Decatur (Center of Hope) serves: Low-income residents / homeless.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Cullman, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
