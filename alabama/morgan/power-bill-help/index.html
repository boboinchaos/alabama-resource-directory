Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Morgan County, Alabama?
Start with Salvation Army of Decatur (Center of Hope) ((256) 353-2822), Beltline Church of Christ Benevolence ((256) 353-1876) and Decatur Utilities - Bill Assistance (LIHEAP & Operation Warm via CAPNA) ((256) 552-1400). This page lists 21 programs that serve Morgan County: 11 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Morgan County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Salvation Army of Decatur (Center of Hope) serves: Low-income residents / homeless.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Cullman, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
