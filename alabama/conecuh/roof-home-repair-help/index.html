USDA 504 repair loans/grants

Local · 7 Alabama counties

1% loans up to $40,000 and grants up to $10,000 (combined $50,000) to repair, modernize or remove health/safety hazards (roofs, plumbing, accessibility).

Who
Very-low/low-income owner-occupants in eligible rural areas unable to get credit elsewhere; grants only for age 62+

Common questions

Where can I get help with roof and home repairs in Conecuh County, Alabama?
Start with USDA Rural Development Section 504 Home Repair - Bay Minette Area Office ((251) 937-3297 ext. 4). This page lists 6 programs that serve Conecuh County: 1 local or regional and 5 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with roof and home repairs in Conecuh County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, USDA Rural Development Section 504 Home Repair - Bay Minette Area Office serves: Very-low/low-income owner-occupants in eligible rural areas unable to get credit elsewhere; grants only for age 62+.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with roof and home repairs near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Butler, Covington, Escambia that may serve you.
