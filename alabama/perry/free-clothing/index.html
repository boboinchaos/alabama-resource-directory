Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Perry County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army Demopolis Service Center (West Alabama Black Belt) ((334) 526-2718). This page lists 3 programs that serve Perry County: 2 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Perry County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Raleigh's Place serves: Foster, kinship and adoptive families and youth aging out in central Alabama.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Chilton, Dallas that may serve you.
