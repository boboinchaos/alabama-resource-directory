Common questions

Where can I get free or low-cost medical and dental care in Crenshaw County, Alabama?
Start with Crenshaw County Health Department (334-335-2471) and SARHA - Dozier Family Health Center ((334) 496-3521). This page lists 10 programs that serve Crenshaw County: 3 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free or low-cost medical and dental care in Crenshaw County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Crenshaw County Health Department serves: Anyone in Crenshaw County; many services sliding-scale or free for low-income residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free or low-cost medical and dental care near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Butler, Coffee, Covington that may serve you.
