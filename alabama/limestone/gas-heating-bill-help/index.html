Common questions

Where can I get help paying a gas or heating bill in Limestone County, Alabama?
Start with Athens Utilities ((256) 232-1440) and The Salvation Army Decatur-Athens Service Center ((256) 353-2822). This page lists 12 programs that serve Limestone County: 5 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a gas or heating bill in Limestone County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Athens Utilities serves: Customers in the service area.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a gas or heating bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Lauderdale, Lawrence, Madison that may serve you.
