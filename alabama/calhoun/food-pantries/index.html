Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Calhoun County, Alabama?
Start with A Servant's Heart Mission ((256) 441-4780), All Saints Interfaith Center of Concern ((256) 236-7793) and Anniston Soup Bowl (256-236-6794). This page lists 33 programs that serve Calhoun County: 27 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Calhoun County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Servant of Jesus Church & Shelter serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Cherokee, Cleburne, Etowah that may serve you.
