Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Clay County, Alabama?
Start with Ashland First United Methodist Church Food Pantry ((256) 354-2267), Clay County Farmers Market Mobile Pantry ((256) 276-1612) and Lineville Baptist Church Food Distribution and Meal ((256) 356-2000). This page lists 14 programs that serve Clay County: 8 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Clay County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Ashland First United Methodist Church Food Pantry serves: Area residents in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Cleburne, Coosa, Randolph that may serve you.
