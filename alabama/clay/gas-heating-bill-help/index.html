The Salvation Army Anniston Corps - Admin Office and Social Services

Local · Calhoun, Clay, Cleburne, Talladega Counties, AL

Emergency assistance and Project SHARE power-bill help; Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 9am-3pm, Wed 9am-2pm.

(256) 236-5643
Who
Low-income households in crisis in the service area; ID, proof of address/income and the past-due bill or lease usually required; aid depends on available funds.

412 Noble St, Anniston 36201

Statewide programs that serve Clay County (6)

LIHEAP energy assistance

Community Action Agency of Talladega, Clay, Randolph, Calhoun & Cleburne Counties

Statewide, AL

Federal energy help paid straight to the power or gas company. Every Alabama county is served by a Community Action Agency that takes the applications.

256-362-6611
Who
Income at or under 150% of the federal poverty level. Heating season: Oct 1 – Apr 30.
Where
136 N Court St, Talladega 35160
Good to know
This is the LIHEAP agency for Clay County (ADECA list).
Bring
  • Photo ID
  • Social Security cards for everyone in the home
  • Last month's income proof for every adult
  • Utility bill in the applicant's name
