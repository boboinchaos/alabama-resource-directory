Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Lawrence County, Alabama?
Start with COC Food Pantry & Soup Kitchen ((256) 227-1674), Foster Outreach Ministries ((256) 476-1047) and Good Samaritan Food Pantry (Good Sam) ((256) 974-7542). This page lists 22 programs that serve Lawrence County: 16 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Lawrence County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, COC Food Pantry & Soup Kitchen serves: Anyone in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Cullman, Franklin that may serve you.
