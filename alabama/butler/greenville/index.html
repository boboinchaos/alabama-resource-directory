Help Paying Power Bills in Greenville

Pioneer Electric Cooperative - Operation Round Up & Medical Priority List

Local · Butler, Dallas, Lowndes, Wilcox Counties, AL

Operation Round Up individual grants for members in need; Medical Priority List for life-support/medical-device users; up to 2 in-person payment arrangements per 12 months; Smart-Pay; Mon-Fri 7am-4pm.

(334) 382-6636
Who
Members of the cooperative; hardship/elderly/disabled members for assistance funds.
Where
300 Herbert Street, Greenville, AL 36037
Good to know
Arrangements must be made in person before last day to pay (not by phone). ORU contact Christi Scruggs (334) 382-4904. Selma office 4075 AL Hwy 41, Sardis 36775, (334) 875-2223. Toll-free (800) 239-3092. Ask the co-op about payment extensions and medical/life-support flags; Alabama PSC Rule 12(E) does not regulate co-op disconnect rules, each co-op's own board policy applies.
