Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Baldwin County, Alabama?
Start with Baldwin EMC - Operation Round Up ((251) 989-6247), Catholic Social Services - Bay Minette ((251) 937-7858) and Catholic Social Services - Bay Minette Office ((251) 937-7858). This page lists 32 programs that serve Baldwin County: 22 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Baldwin County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Baldwin EMC - Operation Round Up serves: Members of the cooperative; hardship/elderly/disabled members for assistance funds.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Clarke, Escambia, Mobile that may serve you.
