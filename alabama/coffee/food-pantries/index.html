Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Coffee County, Alabama?
Start with Beth-El Faith Church ((334) 393-2281), Christian Mission Centers - Elba ((334) 897-6693) and Coffee County Community Church ((334) 806-5955). This page lists 31 programs that serve Coffee County: 25 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Coffee County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Beth-El Faith Church serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Covington, Crenshaw, Dale that may serve you.
