Common questions

Where can I get help paying a gas or heating bill in Coffee County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army - Enterprise (Coffee County) ((334) 393-8244). This page lists 12 programs that serve Coffee County: 5 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a gas or heating bill in Coffee County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, The Salvation Army - Enterprise (Coffee County) serves: Low-income households in crisis in the service area; ID, proof of address/income and the past-due bill or lease usually required; aid depends on available funds.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a gas or heating bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Covington, Crenshaw, Dale that may serve you.
