Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Pike County, Alabama?
Start with Collegedale Church of Christ Food Pantry ((334) 566-5110), First Missionary Baptist Church - Troy ((334) 566-3905) and Salvation Army Service Center - Troy ((334) 808-1069). This page lists 15 programs that serve Pike County: 9 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Pike County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Collegedale Church of Christ Food Pantry serves: Pike County residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Bullock, Coffee that may serve you.
