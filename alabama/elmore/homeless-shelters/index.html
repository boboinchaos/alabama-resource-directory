Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Elmore County, Alabama?
Start with House of Love & Mercy ((334) 283-8116). This page lists 21 programs that serve Elmore County: 9 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Elmore County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Elmore County Community Warming Sites serves: Anyone needing a warm place during freezing weather.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Chilton, Coosa that may serve you.
