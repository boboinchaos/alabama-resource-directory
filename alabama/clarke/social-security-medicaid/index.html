Common questions

Where can I get help with Social Security, SSI or Medicaid in Clarke County, Alabama?
Start with Alabama Tombigbee Area Agency on Aging (334-682-5206). This page lists 23 programs that serve Clarke County: 1 local or regional and 22 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with Social Security, SSI or Medicaid in Clarke County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Alabama Tombigbee Area Agency on Aging serves: Adults 60+, people with disabilities, caregivers.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with Social Security, SSI or Medicaid near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Baldwin, Choctaw, Marengo that may serve you.
