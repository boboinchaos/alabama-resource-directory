Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Colbert County, Alabama?
Start with Colbert Caring Center ((256) 381-8464), Cowboy Church of Colbert County ((256) 810-8773) and Highland Park Church of Christ - Samaritan House ((256) 381-8311). This page lists 22 programs that serve Colbert County: 16 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Colbert County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Colbert Caring Center serves: Colbert County residents; bring ID and proof of residency.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Franklin, Lauderdale, Lawrence that may serve you.
