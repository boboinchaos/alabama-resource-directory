Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Cullman County, Alabama?
Start with Cullman Caring for Kids, Inc. (256-739-1111), First Baptist Church Cullman ((256) 734-5632) and Hanceville Methodist Church (256-352-5536). This page lists 26 programs that serve Cullman County: 20 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Cullman County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Abundant Love Mission - Food Distribution Center serves: Low-income individuals and families; call to confirm requirements (ID/proof of address may be asked).
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Lawrence, Marshall that may serve you.
