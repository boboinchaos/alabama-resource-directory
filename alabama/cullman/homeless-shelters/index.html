Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Cullman County, Alabama?
Start with Cullman County Senior Centers - Daytime Cold-Weather Space ((256) 734-0145), Cullman County Warming Shelters (VOAD network) ((256) 775-7145) and Hanceville Civic Center Warming Station ((256) 595-8931). This page lists 21 programs that serve Cullman County: 9 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Cullman County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Cullman County Senior Centers - Daytime Cold-Weather Space serves: Adults 60+.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Lawrence, Marshall that may serve you.
