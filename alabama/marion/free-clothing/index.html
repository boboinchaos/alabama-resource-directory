Christian Center of Concern - Hamilton

Local · Marion County, AL

Food (one bag per family per month, emergency help), utility payment help about every 3 months, free clothing (4 items/person every 60 days), furniture and household goods; Thursdays 9-11am.

(205) 921-3470
Who
Anyone in need with photo ID; no income limit
Where

The Salvation Army Walker County Service Center

Local · Blount, Cullman, Marion, Marshall, Walker, Winston Counties, AL

Emergency assistance (rent/utilities/food) and Project SHARE power-bill help; call for hours.
