Common questions

Where can I get help with rent or an eviction in Mobile County, Alabama?
Start with Housing Authority of the City of Chickasaw ((251) 457-6841), Housing Authority of the City of Prichard ((251) 283-0111) and Mobile County Housing Authority ((251) 866-9696). This page lists 38 programs that serve Mobile County: 24 local or regional and 14 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with rent or an eviction in Mobile County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Housing Authority of the City of Chickasaw serves: Low-income households under HUD income limits, including older adults and people with disabilities; screening and waiting lists apply.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with rent or an eviction near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Baldwin, Washington that may serve you.
