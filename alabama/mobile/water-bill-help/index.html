Common questions

Where can I get help with a water bill or shutoff in Mobile County, Alabama?
Start with Catholic Social Services (Mobile) ((251) 434-1550), Catholic Social Services - Mobile Service Center (Emergency Assistance) ((251) 434-1500) and Mobile Area Water & Sewer System - Neighbors In Need ((251) 694-3165). This page lists 20 programs that serve Mobile County: 16 local or regional and 4 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with a water bill or shutoff in Mobile County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Catholic Social Services (Mobile) serves: Mobile-area residents in crisis, any faith.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with a water bill or shutoff near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Baldwin, Washington that may serve you.
