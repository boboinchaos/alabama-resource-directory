Common questions

Where can I get help paying a gas or heating bill in Bullock County, Alabama?
Start with OCAP - Bullock County Outreach Office (334-738-3848). This page lists 10 programs that serve Bullock County: 3 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a gas or heating bill in Bullock County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, OCAP - Bullock County Outreach Office serves: Low-income Bullock County households.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a gas or heating bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Macon, Montgomery that may serve you.
