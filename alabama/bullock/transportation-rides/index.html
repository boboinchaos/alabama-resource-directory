Common questions

Where can I get a ride or transportation help in Bullock County, Alabama?
Start with South Central Alabama Area Agency on Aging (334-244-6903). This page lists 9 programs that serve Bullock County: 1 local or regional and 8 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for a ride or transportation help in Bullock County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, South Central Alabama Area Agency on Aging serves: Adults 60+, people with disabilities, caregivers.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for a ride or transportation help near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Barbour, Macon, Montgomery that may serve you.
