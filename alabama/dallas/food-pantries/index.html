Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Dallas County, Alabama?
Start with AGAPE Community Development (334-375-0386), Alabama Avenue Church of God Food Pantry ((334) 874-4420) and Alabama Teen Challenge (334-872-0133). This page lists 42 programs that serve Dallas County: 36 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Dallas County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Abundant Life Center Pantry serves: Selma area residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Chilton, Lowndes that may serve you.
