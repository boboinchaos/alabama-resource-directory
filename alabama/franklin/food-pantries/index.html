Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Franklin County, Alabama?
Start with Faith Mission Outreach ((256) 331-4483), North Highlands Church of Christ ((256) 332-3315) and Russellville Dream Center ((256) 332-4732). This page lists 15 programs that serve Franklin County: 9 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Franklin County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Calvary Baptist Church - Russellville serves: Residents in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Lawrence, Marion that may serve you.
