Common questions

Where can I get free furniture or a bed in Madison County, Alabama?
Start with Christmas Charities Year Round ((256) 837-2373) and Sleep in Heavenly Peace - AL-Huntsville chapter (Huntsville) (844-432-2337). This page lists 6 programs that serve Madison County: 4 local or regional and 2 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free furniture or a bed in Madison County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Christmas Charities Year Round serves: Low-income Madison County residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free furniture or a bed near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Jackson, Limestone, Marshall that may serve you.
