Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Madison County, Alabama?
Start with Downtown Rescue Mission ((256) 536-2441), Salvation Army - Huntsville Corps ((256) 536-5576) and The Salvation Army Huntsville Red Shield Shelter ((256) 536-5576). This page lists 84 programs that serve Madison County: 78 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Madison County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Downtown Rescue Mission serves: People experiencing homelessness.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Jackson, Limestone, Marshall that may serve you.
