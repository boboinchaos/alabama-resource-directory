Common questions

Where can I get a wheelchair, ramp, prosthetic or medical equipment in Madison County, Alabama?
Start with CASA of Madison County ((256) 533-7775) and Enable Madison County ((256) 533-7775). This page lists 39 programs that serve Madison County: 7 local or regional and 32 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for a wheelchair, ramp, prosthetic or medical equipment in Madison County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, CASA of Madison County serves: Seniors and homebound residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for a wheelchair, ramp, prosthetic or medical equipment near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Jackson, Limestone, Marshall that may serve you.
