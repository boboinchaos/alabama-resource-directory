Common questions

Where can I get help paying a gas or heating bill in Madison County, Alabama?
Start with Salvation Army - Huntsville Corps ((256) 536-5576), Huntsville Assistance Program (HAP) ((256) 539-2320) and Huntsville Utilities Project Share ((256) 535-1200). This page lists 17 programs that serve Madison County: 10 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a gas or heating bill in Madison County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Salvation Army - Huntsville Corps serves: Low-income residents; Project SHARE for 62+ or disabled.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a gas or heating bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Jackson, Limestone, Marshall that may serve you.
