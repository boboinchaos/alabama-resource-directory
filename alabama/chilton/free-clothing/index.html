Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Chilton County, Alabama?
Start with Chilton County Emergency Assistance Center ((205) 755-9467), Christ's Closet - Clanton First United Methodist Church ((205) 755-0490) and Son-Light Center and Thrift Shop ((205) 688-2368). This page lists 8 programs that serve Chilton County: 7 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Chilton County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Chilton County Emergency Assistance Center serves: Households under 130% of poverty level; seniors 60+ under 165%.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Bibb, Coosa that may serve you.
