Common questions

Where can I get free or low-cost medical and dental care in Chilton County, Alabama?
Start with Cahaba Medical Care - Maplesville ((334) 366-4040), Health Services, Inc. - Clanton Family Health Center ((205) 755-3980) and Sarrell Dental - Clanton (205-558-9408). This page lists 11 programs that serve Chilton County: 4 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free or low-cost medical and dental care in Chilton County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Cahaba Medical Care - Maplesville serves: Anyone; uninsured/underinsured get sliding-fee discount.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free or low-cost medical and dental care near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Bibb, Coosa that may serve you.
