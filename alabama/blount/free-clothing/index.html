Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Blount County, Alabama?
Start with Blount County Needy School Children ((205) 625-4102) and The Salvation Army - Oneonta (Blount County) ((205) 625-4852). This page lists 4 programs that serve Blount County: 3 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Blount County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Blount County Needy School Children serves: Low-income Blount County schoolchildren.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Cullman, Etowah, Jefferson that may serve you.
