Common questions

Where can I get free or low-cost medical and dental care in Montgomery County, Alabama?
Start with Health Services, Inc. - Chisholm Family Health Center ((334) 832-4338), Health Services, Inc. - Gateway Family Health Center ((334) 288-0009) and Health Services, Inc. - Living Well Specialty Clinic ((334) 420-5024). This page lists 17 programs that serve Montgomery County: 10 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free or low-cost medical and dental care in Montgomery County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Health Services, Inc. - Chisholm Family Health Center serves: Anyone; uninsured/underinsured get sliding-fee discount.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free or low-cost medical and dental care near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Bullock, Crenshaw that may serve you.
