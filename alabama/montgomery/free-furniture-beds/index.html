Common questions

Where can I get free furniture or a bed in Montgomery County, Alabama?
Start with Catholic Social Services of Montgomery ((334) 288-8890), First Baptist Church Community Ministries (Montgomery) ((334) 262-2273) and Helping Hands Thrift Store ((334) 649-2426). This page lists 6 programs that serve Montgomery County: 4 local or regional and 2 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free furniture or a bed in Montgomery County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Catholic Social Services of Montgomery serves: Low-income Montgomery-area residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free furniture or a bed near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Bullock, Crenshaw that may serve you.
