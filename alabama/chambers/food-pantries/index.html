Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Chambers County, Alabama?
Start with Christian Service Center (Lanett) ((334) 576-3552), Circle of Care Center for Families ((334) 768-4091) and Interfaith Food Closet - Lanett UMC ((706) 590-0600). This page lists 18 programs that serve Chambers County: 12 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Chambers County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Bowen East District Ministerial Division serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Lee, Randolph, Tallapoosa that may serve you.
