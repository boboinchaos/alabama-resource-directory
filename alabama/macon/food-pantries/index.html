Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Macon County, Alabama?
Start with Divine Inspirational Ministries Food Pantry ((334) 727-9846), Mary Magdalene Missionary Baptist Church Food Pantry ((334) 727-2923) and The Macon County Food Pantry ((334) 226-0272). This page lists 16 programs that serve Macon County: 10 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Macon County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Divine Inspirational Ministries Food Pantry serves: Macon County residents in need.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bullock, Elmore, Lee that may serve you.
