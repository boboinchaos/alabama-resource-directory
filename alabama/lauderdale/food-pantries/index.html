Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Lauderdale County, Alabama?
Start with Center Star Methodist Church (256-757-3339), Chisholm Hills Church of Christ Benevolence Program ((256) 764-0104) and Cornerstone Church - Central Sharing Center ((256) 980-9172). This page lists 31 programs that serve Lauderdale County: 25 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Lauderdale County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Center Star Methodist Church serves: Anyone in need; call to confirm.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
