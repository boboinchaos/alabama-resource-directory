Common questions

Where can I get respite and caregiver help in Lauderdale County, Alabama?
Start with NACOLG Area Agency on Aging (256-389-0500). This page lists 12 programs that serve Lauderdale County: 1 local or regional and 11 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for respite and caregiver help in Lauderdale County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, NACOLG Area Agency on Aging serves: Adults 60+, people with disabilities, caregivers.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for respite and caregiver help near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
