Common questions

Where can I get help with a water bill or shutoff in Lauderdale County, Alabama?
Start with Florence Utilities - Project Help ((256) 740-6044) and Woodmont Baptist Church Benevolence ((256) 766-1255). This page lists 10 programs that serve Lauderdale County: 6 local or regional and 4 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with a water bill or shutoff in Lauderdale County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Florence Utilities - Project Help serves: Elderly, disabled and others in need in Lauderdale County.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with a water bill or shutoff near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
