Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Lauderdale County, Alabama?
Start with Southern Legacy Assembly Daytime Warming Center. This page lists 22 programs that serve Lauderdale County: 10 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Lauderdale County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Southern Legacy Assembly Daytime Warming Center serves: Anyone needing a warm place during freezing weather.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Colbert, Lawrence, Limestone that may serve you.
