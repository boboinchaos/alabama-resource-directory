Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in St. Clair County, Alabama?
Start with Coosa Valley Electric Cooperative - Lights of Hope ((256) 362-4180), Project SHARE (Alabama Power / Salvation Army) - statewide line ((205) 328-2420 ext. 2) and The Salvation Army Birmingham Area Command (Social Services) ((205) 328-2420). This page lists 14 programs that serve St. Clair County: 4 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in St. Clair County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, ABC Trust (Alabama Business Charitable Trust Fund) serves: Low-income families and households facing temporary financial problems.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Calhoun, Etowah that may serve you.
