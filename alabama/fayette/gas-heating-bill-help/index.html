LIHEAP energy assistance

Community Service Programs of West Alabama

Statewide, AL

Federal energy help paid straight to the power or gas company. Every Alabama county is served by a Community Action Agency that takes the applications.

833-836-7817
Who
Income at or under 150% of the federal poverty level. Heating season: Oct 1 – Apr 30.
Where
601 Black Bears Way, Tuscaloosa 35401
Good to know
Office line: 205-752-5429. Clients 60 and older can call 205-469-0386. This is the LIHEAP agency for Fayette County (ADECA list).

  • Social Security cards for everyone in the home
  • Last month's income proof for every adult

Common questions

Where can I get help paying a gas or heating bill in Fayette County, Alabama?
Start with LIHEAP energy assistance (833-836-7817), Public Service Commission consumer complaints (1-800-392-8050) and Spire Alabama assistance (1-800-292-4008). This page lists 7 programs that serve Fayette County: 0 local or regional and 7 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a gas or heating bill in Fayette County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Alabama Public Service Commission - utility complaints & disconnect rules serves: Customers of investor-owned utilities (not co-ops, municipal or most water boards).
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a gas or heating bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Lamar, Marion, Pickens that may serve you.
