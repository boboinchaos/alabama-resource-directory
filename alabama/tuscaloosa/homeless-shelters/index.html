Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army Tuscaloosa Corps ((205) 632-3691), Tracy Dent Foundation - Dent Transitional Program ((205) 246-5655) and Tuscaloosa County EMA Warming Station ((205) 349-0150). This page lists 18 programs that serve Tuscaloosa County: 6 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Tuscaloosa County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, The Salvation Army Tuscaloosa Corps serves: Tuscaloosa-area residents in need; financial help about once every 90 days.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Fayette, Greene that may serve you.
