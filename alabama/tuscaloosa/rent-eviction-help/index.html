Common questions

Where can I get help with rent or an eviction in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army Tuscaloosa Corps ((205) 632-3691), West Alabama Coalition for the Homeless ((205) 248-5080) and Northport Housing Authority ((205) 752-8171). This page lists 26 programs that serve Tuscaloosa County: 12 local or regional and 14 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with rent or an eviction in Tuscaloosa County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, The Salvation Army Tuscaloosa Corps serves: Tuscaloosa-area residents in need; financial help about once every 90 days.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with rent or an eviction near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Bibb, Fayette, Greene that may serve you.
