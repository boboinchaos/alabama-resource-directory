Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Escambia County, Alabama?
Start with Project SHARE intake - Community Action Agency of South Alabama - Brewton (251-867-4759). This page lists 18 programs that serve Escambia County: 8 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Escambia County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Project SHARE intake - Community Action Agency of South Alabama - Brewton serves: Low-income residents age 60+ and/or disabled; bring ID, bill/cutoff notice, proof of income.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Baldwin, Conecuh, Covington that may serve you.
