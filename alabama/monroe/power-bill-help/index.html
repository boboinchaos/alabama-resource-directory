Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Monroe County, Alabama?
Start with American Red Cross - Monroe County Chapter ((251) 544-6100) and Community Action Agency of South Alabama - Monroeville Office (251-743-3137). This page lists 17 programs that serve Monroe County: 7 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Monroe County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, American Red Cross - Monroe County Chapter serves: Monroe County residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Baldwin, Butler, Clarke that may serve you.
