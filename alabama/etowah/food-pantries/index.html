Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Etowah County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army - Gadsden ((256) 546-4673), Antioch Baptist Church Food Pantry ((256) 492-5521) and Antioch United Methodist Church Mobile Pantry ((256) 312-5652). This page lists 36 programs that serve Etowah County: 30 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Etowah County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, The Salvation Army - Gadsden serves: Etowah County residents in need; bring ID and proof of income.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Calhoun, Cherokee that may serve you.
