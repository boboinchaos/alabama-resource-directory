Common questions

Where can I get help with a water bill or shutoff in Etowah County, Alabama?
Start with Catholic Center of Concern - Gadsden ((256) 546-0028), Gadsden Water Works - Water Assistance Program ((256) 543-2884) and The Salvation Army Gadsden Corps ((256) 546-4673). This page lists 7 programs that serve Etowah County: 3 local or regional and 4 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with a water bill or shutoff in Etowah County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Catholic Center of Concern - Gadsden serves: Low-income individuals and families regardless of religion; call for intake.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with a water bill or shutoff near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Calhoun, Cherokee that may serve you.
