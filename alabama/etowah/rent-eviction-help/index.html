Common questions

Where can I get help with rent or an eviction in Etowah County, Alabama?
Start with Homeless Coalition of Northeast Alabama ((256) 549-4532), The Salvation Army - Gadsden ((256) 546-4673) and Greater Gadsden Housing Authority ((256) 547-2501). This page lists 24 programs that serve Etowah County: 10 local or regional and 14 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help with rent or an eviction in Etowah County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Homeless Coalition of Northeast Alabama serves: People who are homeless or at risk.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help with rent or an eviction near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Blount, Calhoun, Cherokee that may serve you.
