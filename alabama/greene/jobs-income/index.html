Common questions

Where can I get job and income help in Greene County, Alabama?
Start with ADRS Vocational Rehabilitation (equipment for work) (1-800-441-7607), United Way 2-1-1 (2-1-1) and AlabamaWorks Career Centers (334-309-9000). This page lists 9 programs that serve Greene County: 0 local or regional and 9 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for job and income help in Greene County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, ADRS Vocational Rehabilitation (equipment for work) serves: Alabamians with a disability who want to work.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Social Security card, Resume or work history, if you have one. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for job and income help near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Hale, Marengo, Pickens that may serve you.
