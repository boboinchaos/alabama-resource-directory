Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Lowndes County, Alabama?
Start with Bethlehem Christian Church Food Pantry ((334) 404-9838), Color Full Visions ((334) 300-7005) and First Missionary Baptist Church - Lowndesboro ((334) 875-6099). This page lists 17 programs that serve Lowndes County: 11 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Lowndes County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Bethlehem Christian Church Food Pantry serves: Fort Deposit area residents.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Autauga, Butler, Crenshaw that may serve you.
