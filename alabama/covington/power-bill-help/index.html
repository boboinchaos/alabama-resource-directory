Common questions

Where can I get help paying a power bill in Covington County, Alabama?
Start with Project SHARE intake - Organized Community Action Program (OCAP) - Covington County (334-428-2660), Salvation Army (Andalusia office) ((334) 222-0112) and SVdP - Christ the King Conference (Andalusia) ((334) 222-4858). This page lists 24 programs that serve Covington County: 14 local or regional and 10 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for help paying a power bill in Covington County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Project SHARE intake - Organized Community Action Program (OCAP) - Covington County serves: Low-income residents age 60+ and/or disabled; bring ID, bill/cutoff notice, proof of income.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, The past-due bill or shutoff notice, Proof of income for the last 30 days (pay stubs, award letters), Social Security cards or numbers for everyone in the home. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for help paying a power bill near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Butler, Coffee, Conecuh that may serve you.
