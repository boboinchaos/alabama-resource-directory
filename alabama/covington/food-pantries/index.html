Common questions

Where can I get free food and food pantries in Covington County, Alabama?
Start with Andalusia Adult Activity Center ((334) 222-6891), Andalusia Full Gospel Tabernacle (334-222-8353) and Christian Service Center (Andalusia) ((334) 222-3257). This page lists 25 programs that serve Covington County: 19 local or regional and 6 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free food and food pantries in Covington County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Andalusia Adult Activity Center serves: Seniors.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free food and food pantries near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Butler, Coffee, Conecuh that may serve you.
