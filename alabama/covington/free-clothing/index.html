Common questions

Where can I get free clothing in Covington County, Alabama?
Start with The Salvation Army - Andalusia office (Covington County) ((334) 222-0112). This page lists 7 programs that serve Covington County: 6 local or regional and 1 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for free clothing in Covington County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Angel's Attic - Central Church of Christ (Andalusia) serves: Foster families in Covington County.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for free clothing near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Butler, Coffee, Conecuh that may serve you.
