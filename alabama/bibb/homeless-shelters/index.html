Common questions

Where can I get emergency shelter in Bibb County, Alabama?
Start with Bibb County Warming Sites. This page lists 19 programs that serve Bibb County: 7 local or regional and 12 statewide or national.
Who qualifies for emergency shelter in Bibb County?
Every program sets its own rules, so read the Who line on each listing. For example, Bibb County Warming Sites serves: Anyone needing a warm place during freezing weather.
What should I bring when I apply?
Most programs ask for: Photo ID, Proof of address (lease, mortgage statement or a bill), Proof of income for the last 30 days. Call first and ask what that office needs, since it changes.
What if every program says it is out of money?
Funding opens and closes, often at the start of the month. Dial 2-1-1 (free, 24/7) and ask for emergency shelter near your address, and check programs in neighboring counties like Chilton, Hale, Jefferson that may serve you.
