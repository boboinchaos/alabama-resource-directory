Got a Water Shutoff Notice in Alabama? What to Do Today

By the Alabama Resource Directory team · Updated November 2, 2026

Water help is harder to find than power help because there's no big federal program just for water. But most water systems and many charities do help. Here is what to do, in order.

1. Call your water company before the cutoff date

Ask for a payment arrangement or extension. Most water boards would rather get paid over time than shut you off. If your bill jumped because of a leak, ask about a leak adjustment. Many utilities credit part of the bill once the leak is fixed.

2. Ask about the utility's own help fund

Several Alabama water systems run assistance funds, often with a local partner:

  • Central Alabama Water (Birmingham area): the H2O Foundation, 205-244-4390, can pay up to $500 toward a bill and up to $1,000 for small plumbing repairs.
  • MAWSS (Mobile): Neighbors In Need, screened through Mobile Community Action.
  • Gadsden Water Works: a Water Assistance Program through Community Action of Etowah County.
  • Florence Utilities: Project Help pays up to $100 toward final-notice bills.
  • Decatur Utilities: Operation Warm and LIHEAP through Community Action Partnership of North Alabama.

3. Local charities

Churches, the Salvation Army and St. Vincent de Paul often help with water bills, usually once a year. In Lee County, Presbyterian Community Ministry makes zero-interest emergency loans up to $300 for water, gas or power bills. Your county's water bill help page lists who is active near you.

4. Dial 2-1-1

2-1-1 tracks smaller funds that open and close each month. Call, or text your ZIP code to 898-211.

Who regulates water shutoffs?

Most city and county water boards in Alabama are not regulated by the Public Service Commission, so complaints go to the water board itself or your city council. Be polite, put requests in writing, and keep copies.

If the problem is a leak or broken pipe

A running toilet or hidden leak can double a bill. Fixing it is usually cheaper than paying for the wasted water. See plumbing repair help for free repair programs, or get a plumber's quote.

Every program changes. Call before you go, and dial 2-1-1 for anything not listed. See something wrong? Tell us.
