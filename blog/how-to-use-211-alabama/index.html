How 2-1-1 Works in Alabama (and How to Get More Out of the Call)

By the Alabama Resource Directory team · Updated November 1, 2026

2-1-1 is a free, confidential help line that connects you to local programs for food, bills, housing, shelters and more. It works from any phone in Alabama, 24 hours a day.

Three ways to reach it

  • Call 2-1-1, or 1-888-421-1266 if 2-1-1 doesn't connect from your phone
  • Text your ZIP code to 898-211
  • Search online at 211connectsalabama.org

What 2-1-1 does well

2-1-1 specialists keep track of small funds that open and close week to week: church funds, one-time grants and seasonal programs that are hard to find online. If every program you've called is out of money, 2-1-1 is the next call.

What it doesn't do

2-1-1 doesn't pay bills or decide who qualifies. It gives you names and numbers. You still have to call and apply.

Say this to get better referrals

  1. Your ZIP code and county
  2. The exact problem and deadline ("Alabama Power shutoff on Friday, $312 past due")
  3. Anyone in the home who is 60+, disabled, pregnant or a child under 5. Many programs put these households first.
  4. What you've already tried, so they don't send you back to the same places

Use this directory alongside 2-1-1

Every county page here lists programs with phone numbers, who qualifies and what to bring, so you can start calling before or after your 2-1-1 call. Pick your county or search all programs.

In a mental health crisis, call or text 988. In an emergency, call 911.

Every program changes. Call before you go, and dial 2-1-1 for anything not listed. See something wrong? Tell us.
