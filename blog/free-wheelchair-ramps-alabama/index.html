How to Get a Free Wheelchair Ramp in Alabama

By the Alabama Resource Directory team · Updated November 3, 2026

A ramp is often the difference between being stuck inside and getting to the doctor, church or the mailbox. Across Alabama, volunteers and public programs build ramps for free. Here is where to start.

Statewide: ADRS SAIL

Call the Alabama Department of Rehabilitation Services' SAIL program at 1-844-602-7245. If you have a significant disability, an Independent Living specialist can assess your home. Ramps are often built by local church or Lions Club volunteers with materials ADRS supplies.

Volunteer ramp ministries

These groups build ramps at no cost. Most screen by need, not church membership:

  • Men on Missions (Geneva, Houston and nearby counties): free ramps plus porch, step and handrail repairs
  • First United Methodist Church Brewton Men's Ministry (Escambia County and south Alabama): 500+ ramps built
  • Carpenters for Christ, First Baptist Tallassee (Elmore and Tallapoosa): ramps for ADRS clients
  • Clarke County Kingdom Builders (Clarke County)
  • Enable Madison County (Huntsville/Madison County): new ramps, repairs and fall prevention
  • Repair Baldwin (Baldwin County): ramps along with other critical repairs

County accessibility grants

Some counties pay for ramps, grab bars and wider doors through federal CDBG money. Jefferson County Community Services (205-325-5761) offers Housing Accessibility grants to income-eligible homeowners outside Birmingham, Bessemer and a few other cities.

Older homeowners

If you are 62 or older and live in a rural area, a USDA Section 504 grant (up to $10,000) can cover a ramp and bathroom safety work. Your Area Agency on Aging can also point you to local ramp programs.

What to have ready

  • A note from a doctor or therapist about mobility needs, if you have one
  • Proof you own the home, or written permission from your landlord
  • A rough idea of how many steps are at your entrance (this sets the ramp length: about 1 foot of ramp for every inch of rise)

Paying for one yourself

If the waiting lists are too long, a licensed contractor can build a code-compliant wood or aluminum ramp, usually in a few days. Request free ramp quotes here. Aluminum modular ramps can also be rented short-term after a surgery.
